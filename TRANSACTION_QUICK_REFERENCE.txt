🎯 SYSTEM OVERVIEW
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The transaction system automatically records ALL inventory and sales activities
in the database. Every transaction is timestamped and attributed to the staff
member who recorded it.

📊 TWO MAIN TRANSACTION TYPES
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1️⃣ INVENTORY TRANSACTIONS (inventory_transactions table)
   Purpose: Track movement of products and materials
   
   Types:
   • IN        - Stock received from suppliers
   • OUT       - Items sold to customers
   • ADJUSTMENT - Corrections for damage, loss, or discrepancies
   
   Records:
   ├─ What: Product/Material name
   ├─ How Much: Quantity affected
   ├─ Why: Notes explaining the transaction
   ├─ Who: Staff member (created_by)
   ├─ When: Date and time (created_at)
   └─ Link: To products or inventory_materials

2️⃣ SALES TRANSACTIONS (sales_transactions table)
   Purpose: Record all money coming in from sales
   
   Records:
   ├─ Amount: Total sales value
   ├─ Date: When the sale occurred
   ├─ Notes: Order details
   ├─ Who: Staff member who recorded it
   ├─ When: Date and time recorded
   └─ Link: To orders (if applicable)


🔄 HOW DATA FLOWS IN THE SYSTEM
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

WHEN ITEMS ARE RECEIVED (Stock In):
  1. Staff goes to: Staff > Inventory > Add Item
  2. Enters product, quantity, supplier details
  3. Clicks Submit
  4. ✅ System creates:
     - Inventory transaction (Type: IN)
     - Updates product quantity
     - Records who did it and when


WHEN ITEMS ARE SOLD (Stock Out):
  1. Staff goes to: Staff > Sales > Create Sale
  2. Selects items and quantities
  3. Clicks Submit
  4. ✅ System creates:
     - Sale record (sales table)
     - Sale items (sale_items table) 
     - Inventory 'OUT' transaction for each item
     - Sales transaction with total amount
     - Updates inventory quantities
     - Records staff member and timestamps


WHEN INVENTORY NEEDS CORRECTING (Adjustment):
  1. Staff records wrong quantity or damage
  2. Staff goes to: Staff > Inventory > Add Transaction
  3. Selects "Adjustment" type
  4. Enters quantity (negative for reductions)
  5. ✅ System creates:
     - Inventory transaction (Type: ADJUSTMENT)
     - Records reason in notes
     - Updates quantity

🗄️ DATABASE TABLES
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

inventory_transactions:
  id ..................... Primary key (unique ID)
  product_id ............. Link to products table
  material_id ............ Link to materials table
  transaction_type ....... 'in', 'out', or 'adjustment'
  quantity ............... Units affected
  notes .................. Reason/details
  created_by ............. User ID who recorded it
  created_at ............. Timestamp when recorded

sales_transactions:
  id ..................... Primary key (unique ID)
  order_id ............... Link to orders table
  transaction_date ....... Date of sale
  total_sales ............ Amount earned (₱)
  notes .................. Order details
  created_by ............. User ID who recorded it
  created_at ............. Timestamp when recorded

sales:
  id ..................... Primary key
  sale_date .............. When sale occurred
  total_amount ........... Total value
  created_at ............. When recorded

sale_items:
  id ..................... Primary key
  sale_id ................ Link to sales table
  inventory_id ........... Product sold
  quantity ............... How many units
  unit_price ............. Price per unit
  subtotal ............... Total for this item

🎯 BEST PRACTICES
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. ALWAYS ADD DETAILED NOTES
   Explain WHY the transaction happened
   Examples:
   • "Received from Star Paper Corporation"
   • "Sold to Smith Motor Trade customer"
   • "Damaged during handling - removed from stock"
   • "Printing job for Company XYZ"

2. RECORD IMMEDIATELY
   Don't wait - record transactions as they happen
   Ensures accurate real-time inventory

3. USE CORRECT TYPES
   • IN for received items
   • OUT for sold items
   • ADJUSTMENT for corrections

4. VERIFY OFTEN
   Use the Transactions page to check accuracy

5. REVIEW REPORTS
   Check statistics weekly for trends

📞 QUICK ANSWERS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q: When are transactions recorded?
A: Immediately when created. Staff records, system timestamps it.

Q: Who can see transactions?
A: Staff sees their own in transactions page. Admin sees all.

Q: Can transactions be deleted?
A: No - they're permanent for audit trail purposes.

Q: How do I create a sales transaction?
A: Go to Staff > Sales > Create - it's automatic when you submit.

Q: How do I know what sold?
A: Look at the Sales Transactions page for revenue records.

Q: How do I track inventory movement?
A: Look at Inventory Transactions page with type filters.

Q: Are transactions backed up?
A: Yes - they're in the MySQL database with automatic backups.

Q: Can I export transactions?
A: You can see them on screen, copy from table, or generate reports.
